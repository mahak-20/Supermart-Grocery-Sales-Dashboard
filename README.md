# Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard

An interactive business intelligence dashboard built to analyze retail sales data[cite: 1]. This project converts raw grocery sales records into an executive-ready visual interface, enabling management to track sales, profitability, product performance, discount impacts, and regional trends without digging through the full dataset[cite: 1].

---

## Project Context

This dashboard addresses a core business need: giving management a single, easy-to-understand view of overall store performance[cite: 1]. Rather than manually inspecting spreadsheets, stakeholders can use interactive visuals to quickly answer key operational and financial questions[cite: 1].

---

## Business Objectives & Analytical Scope

The dashboard is structured around four core business objectives[cite: 2, 3, 4, 5]:

### 1. Overall Sales Performance[cite: 2]
* **Core Question:** *How is the overall grocery business performing?*[cite: 2]
* **Key Metrics & Tasks:**
  * Track total sales and total profit[cite: 2].
  * Identify product categories contributing the largest share of total sales[cite: 2].
  * Analyze sales trends and changes over time[cite: 2].
  * Pinpoint regions generating the highest sales volume[cite: 2].

### 2. Product Performance[cite: 3]
* **Core Question:** *Which products and categories are driving sales and profit?*[cite: 3]
* **Key Metrics & Tasks:**
  * Rank top-performing categories and sub-categories by sales[cite: 3].
  * Identify high-revenue products that suffer from comparatively low profit margins[cite: 3].

### 3. Profit & Discount Analysis[cite: 4]
* **Core Question:** *How are discounts and profit related to business performance?*[cite: 4]
* **Key Metrics & Tasks:**
  * Determine which categories and regions generate the highest overall profit[cite: 4].
  * Evaluate the correlation between discount rates and profitability[cite: 4].

### 4. Geographic Performance[cite: 5]
* **Core Question:** *Which locations are contributing most to the business?*[cite: 5]
* **Key Metrics & Tasks:**
  * Identify top-performing states and cities by sales[cite: 5].
  * Spot locations with strong sales volume but disproportionately low profit[cite: 5].
